The Sunforged Edge
Objective Type: Infiltration & Retrieval / Combat
Quest Giver: Sir Alistair (Dignified yet desperate retired knight commander)

Narrative Hook: The ancestral sword Sunforge, symbol of the Silverguard Order and key to the citadel's sealed vault, was stolen under cover of darkness by a rogue thief guild. Sir Alistair fears that if the blade is not recovered before sunrise, the vault will be breached and its ancient curse unleashed upon the realm.

Mission Steps:

Track the thieves' trail through the misty lowlands to their hideout in the Sunken Catacombs.
Infiltrate the catacombs while disarming hidden mechanical traps and evading perimeter sentinels.
Defeat Thief Captain Vane in the subterranean chamber and retrieve the Sunforge.
Return the Sunforge to Sir Alistair at the citadel shrine before the first light of dawn.
The Twist: Upon confronting Captain Vane, he reveals he was secretly hired by Sir Alistair's own trusted lieutenant, who planned to use the theft to frame Alistair for treason and seize control of the Order. The player must decide whether to bring Vane in alive to expose the lieutenant or quiet him and return the blade cleanly.

Rewards:

1,200 Gold Coins & 3,500 XP
Relic: Sunforged Signet Ring (+15 Radiance Resistance)
Favor of the Silverguard (+500 Faction Reputation)
