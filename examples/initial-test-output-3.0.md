### The Blackthorn Blade

**Objective Type:** Retrieval / Combat
**Quest Giver:** Sir Aldric Wynn (Honorable but haunted)
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Recommended Level:** 12-16
**Setting:** A fog-drenched marshland fortress overtaken by brigands, where crumbling stone towers loom above black water and the air reeks of smoke and decay.

**Narrative Hook:**
The legendary sword Blackthorn, symbol of the kingdom's rightful bloodline, has been stolen from the royal vault by a rogue knight and spirited away to a marsh fortress. Without the blade's return before the coronation moon, the new queen's claim to the throne will be challenged and civil war will consume the realm.

**Mission Steps:**

1. Traverse the Greymire Marshes, navigating hidden trails to reach the outer walls of Fenhollow Fortress without alerting the brigand sentries.
2. Infiltrate the fortress courtyard and defeat the brigand captain guarding the inner keep gate.
3. Retrieve the Blackthorn Blade from the locked reliquary chamber deep within the keep's lower halls.
4. Escape the fortress through the flooded tunnels before the rogue knight's reinforcements seal every exit.

**The Twist:**
Upon seizing the sword, the rogue knight — Sir Caedan — confronts the player and reveals the blade was taken to prevent a corrupt advisor from using its enchantment to control the new queen. The player must choose: return the sword to the crown as promised, securing the queen's authority but leaving her vulnerable to the advisor's manipulation, or surrender it to Sir Caedan, who will destroy the enchantment but brand the player a traitor in the eyes of the court. Returning the blade earns royal favor and wealth; siding with Caedan earns the loyalty of a secret knightly order and a powerful ally.

**Rewards:**

- 2,500 Gold Crowns & 3,000 XP
- Blackthorn Signet Ring (Unique accessory: +15 Authority, unlocks noble dialogue options)
- Faction Consequence: +400 Royal Court Reputation if blade returned, or +400 Order of the Veil Reputation if blade surrendered to Caedan
