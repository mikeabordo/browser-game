### The Stolen Sunblade

**Objective Type:** Retrieval / Investigation
**Quest Giver:** Lord Roderick (Desperate and honor-bound)
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Recommended Level:** 12-16
**Setting:** A mist-shrouded mountain hold crumbling under the shadow of ancient, moss-covered stone ramparts.

**Narrative Hook:**
The ancestral blade Sunfall was stolen from the high vault during the midnight feast of the sol-king. Without the blade's blessing to sanctify the upcoming solstice rites, Lord Roderick's line will be disgraced and war will erupt between the highland clans.

**Mission Steps:**

1. Investigate the ancestral vault for clues left behind by the intruder.
2. Track the thief through the treacherous Ironwood Pass using forgotten trail signs.
3. Infiltrate the sunken ruins where the thief's hideout is concealed.
4. Confront the thief master before the sun sets beneath the horizon.

**The Twist:**
Upon confronting the thief, the player discovers she is Roderick's estranged sister, who claims the blade was illegally forged with stolen souls and holds the curse that slays their bloodline. The player must choose to return the blade to Roderick to prevent war while hiding the curse, or shatter the sword to break the family curse at the cost of igniting a clan war. Returning the sword earns heavy honor and gold from Roderick, while destroying it grants ancient relic shards and earns the sister's allegiance.

**Rewards:**

- 800 Gold Pieces & 2,500 XP
- Ring of the Solstice (Grants +10 Light Damage resistance)
- Highland Clan Honor (+250 Reputation with Clan Roderick or +250 Reputation with the Outcast Sister)
